2. Third-Party Services

BillKaro uses the following third-party services to provide its features:

Google Gemini AI

  • Used for: Chat processing, invoice creation, purchase bill extraction, voice transcription, HSN code resolution, munshi insights
  • What's sent: Text messages, invoice details, images of purchase bills, audio recordings
  • Data retention: Google processes data in real-time and does not store it for model training under the API terms
  • Google Gemini API Terms

Supabase (Database & Storage)

  • Used for: Storing all business data, invoices, inventory, user accounts
  • Location: Cloud servers (AWS)
  • Security: Row-level security, encrypted at rest
  • Supabase Privacy Policy

Sandbox.co.in (GST APIs)

  • Used for: GSTIN verification, GST return filing, e-invoice generation, e-way bill (paid plans only)
  • What's sent: GSTIN numbers, invoice data for filing, business registration details
  • This is a GSTN-authorized Tax Suvidha Provider
  • Sandbox Privacy Policy

Google Sign-In

  • Used for: Authentication only
  • We receive: Email address and display name
  • We do NOT access: Contacts, calendar, drive, or any other Google data

Firebase App Distribution

  • Used for: Beta testing distribution only
  • Firebase Privacy Policy

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do NOT sell your data to anyone
  • We do NOT use your data for advertising
  • We do NOT share your business data with other users
  • We do NOT store your GST portal password (only time-limited tokens)
  • We do NOT access your bank account or UPI transactions
  • We do NOT train AI models on your data

4. Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Database encrypted at rest (Supabase/AWS)
  • GST credentials stored with encryption
  • Row-level security ensures users can only access their own data
  • API keys and secrets stored as environment variables, never in code

5. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data in the app at any time
  • Export: GST reports, invoices, and khata data can be exported/shared
  • Delete: Contact us to delete your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent: You can stop using AI features by uninstalling the app

6. Data Retention

  • Business data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Invoice and filing records are retained for 8 years (GST compliance requirement)
  • Voice recordings are processed in real-time and not stored after transcription
  • Deleted accounts: data removed within 30 days

7. Children

BillKaro is a business tool intended for use by adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as we add features. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest version.
